Our commitments, kept on every trip

Salaried drivers, GPS tracking, dealer maintenance, comprehensive insurance: concrete commitments that we write into our haulage contracts.

Salaried drivers

Drivers employed by LIS GROUP, trained and fitted with PPE, briefed on the HSE rules and traffic plan of every site.

GPS tracking

Every truck is tracked: position, loading and turnaround times monitored continuously and shared with you on request.

Dealer maintenance

Preventive maintenance at the manufacturer’s dealer, following the manufacturer’s schedule, to keep downtime low.

Comprehensive insurance

Vehicles, goods carried and third-party liability covered by comprehensive (all-risks) insurance.

Legal axle loads

No overloading: we stay within the limits of WAEMU Regulation 14/2005, for safety and for the roads.

Every trip traceable

Delivery note and weighbridge ticket for every trip, with a monthly summary of all rotations.

A Senegalese company, for Senegalese industry

Capital, management, drivers and purchasing are Senegalese. Working with LIS GROUP increases the share of national suppliers in your spend, in the spirit of the national local content policy.
